# THE TIBETAN BOOK OF THE DEAD

Five deaths. Five increasingly ridiculous attempts to escape saṃsāra.

**You are dead.**

Oh, for fuck’s sake.

Again.

Now, before you start complaining, we need to establish something rather important.

According to Tibetan Buddhism, **you have been dying for a very, very long time.**

Not metaphorically. Not just in the poetic sense that your childhood has vanished or that yesterday's version of you no longer exists.

Quite literally, according to this tradition, there has been an inconceivably long sequence of births, deaths, experiences, attachments, terrors, desires and identities.

And every single time, you have mistaken the latest temporary arrangement for *yourself*\.

You've been a human. Perhaps an animal. Perhaps a god. Perhaps something considerably less pleasant.

Every time, consciousness arises under conditions, constructs a world, identifies with some part of it, and eventually loses the particular body supporting that experience.

And what does it do?

**It tries to do the whole fucking thing again.**

The Tibetan Book of the Dead is essentially a manual for interrupting this extraordinary administrative disaster.

Its central instruction is remarkably simple\:

**Recognize what is happening before you become convinced it is happening to someone.**

This five-chapter reading will follow one fictional karmic continuum through five very different deaths. Each lifetime changes the conditions of the next encounter with death. The comedy is original; the doctrinal machinery is grounded in the Tibetan sources.

## The five-death structure

# I

**You Are Dead. And You Were an Absolute Bastard.**

The eight dissolutions, clear light, karmic momentum, the six realms, hell, and why there is no cosmic judge to complain to.

---

# II

**You're Dead Again. Please Stop Running Away from the Buddhas.**

The six bardos, peaceful and wrathful deities, visionary luminosity, the forty-nine-day framework, the bardo body, womb-entry and how recognition interrupts rebirth.

---

# III

**You Fed Some Geckos, Chanted at Them, and Now You're Dead.**

Merit, dedication, bodhicitta, liberation through hearing and seeing, mantra, blessed objects, karma's causal mechanics, Amitābha's Sukhāvatī, Maitreya's Tuṣita, and why a pleasant heaven is not necessarily liberation.

---

# IV

**You Actually Meditated This Time. Unfortunately, You're Still Dead.**

Death yoga, phowa, the subtlest mind, winds and channels, mother-and-child luminosity, tukdam, delayed decomposition, gamma synchrony, bioelectric coherence and Michael Levin's theories of patterns and agency.

---

# V

**You're Dead. Except There Is No Longer Anyone to Die.**

Dzogchen, rigpa, trekchö, tögal, rainbow body (not a political alliance), the three kāyas, liberation at death, Buddha-fields and the deliberately compassionate return of awakened beings.
